Research

My research centers on reasoning in large multimodal models, along two complementary directions.

Efficient reasoning in vision-language models

Test-time scaling, chain-of-thought compression, and verifier-based / speculative decoding — making strong multimodal reasoning cheap enough to deploy.

Reasoning in knowledge-intensive settings

Long-form video understanding, multi-hop visual QA, and tool-augmented multimodal agents. I am especially interested in bridging training-time alignment (RL / GRPO) with inference-time reasoning, and in understanding when multimodal models can reliably reason beyond their pretraining distribution.
