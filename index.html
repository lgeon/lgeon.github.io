Research

My research centers on reasoning in MLLMs: how they can actively reason over long, complex inputs and external knowledge, rather than relying on a single-pass snapshot of what they see.

Agentic multimodal reasoning.

Models that actively gather evidence while reasoning — interacting with the visual input, retrieving external knowledge (multimodal RAG), and calling tools — across long-form video understanding, multi-hop visual question answering, and tool-augmented agents, rather than settling for a single-pass perceptual snapshot.

Efficient multimodal reasoning.

Test-time scaling, chain-of-thought compression, and verifier-based / speculative decoding — making strong multimodal reasoning cheap enough to actually deploy.
